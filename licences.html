Licences and data sources

Traces blanches assembles public data. Each source keeps its licence and its credit. This page lists them, then gives the licences of the code and of this website.

Principles

  • One layer per source. A pack is a set of independent layers. Each layer keeps the licence of its source, written in its metadata and in the pack manifest.
  • No mixing of incompatible licences. Data from OpenStreetMap (ODbL) and data from refuges.info or Camptocamp (CC BY-SA) are never merged for the same kind of feature.
  • Our own computations (slopes, avalanche exposure, estimated snow, no-signal areas) are published under the Licence Ouverte 2.0, with the credit of their sources.
  • The data pipeline code is published. That is the offer the ODbL asks for, for the layers derived from OpenStreetMap.
  • The app shows the credits: “© OpenStreetMap” in a corner of the map, the others on a “Data licences” screen. planned

Sources kept out of published packs

Some sources do not allow redistribution. The data pipeline reads them only for personal use, behind an option that is off by default, and puts them in no published pack:

  • trail status from the Espace Nordique Jurassien and Nordic France, and the grooming page of the Cervières town hall: no reuse licence; we will ask for written permission;
  • the map of recorded avalanches (CLPA) and the permanent avalanche survey (EPA) on the INRAE website: restricted to personal use;
  • the map of recorded avalanches republished by the Hautes-Alpes DDT under the Licence Ouverte: the DDT must first confirm in writing its right to republish it;
  • the Biodiv'sports areas: no licence found; permission from the LPO is still to be requested;
  • the free Open-Meteo API, restricted to non-commercial use.

Trail maps drawn by ski resorts are never redistributed, as images, as layers or in the repository.

Code

  • The code is under the GPL-3.0-or-later, with an additional permission that allows distribution through app stores (App Store, Google Play).
  • The avalanche exposure computation (crates/avalanche) is a port of com4FlowPy and stays under the EUPL-1.2. It runs in the data pipeline; the phone only receives the computed layer.
  • Third-party libraries are under permissive licences (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD and similar). Their list will be on the app's “Licences” screen. planned

Maps on this site

The maps on this site are renders from the project's viewer, with the cross-country, backcountry and guidance styles, on the Cervières and Chapelle-des-Bois packs. Each caption carries a short credit; here are the full sources.

  • Base map: IGN, Plan IGN v2, Licence Ouverte 2.0.
  • Slopes and terrain: IGN, LiDAR HD terrain model, Licence Ouverte 2.0.
  • Trails: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, daily OpenSnowMap extract; Nordic centres: Protomaps © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Huts: © refuges.info contributors, CC BY-SA.
  • No-signal areas: ARCEP, Mon Réseau Mobile, maps of 30 June 2026, Licence Ouverte.
  • Snow: Météo-France, snowpack simulation of 14 November 2026, Licence Ouverte 2.0; massif outlines from snowtools (CNRM), CeCILL-C.

No map on this site shows a source restricted to personal use, the map of recorded avalanches (CLPA) or the Biodiv'sports areas. The render script hides these layers before each capture.
