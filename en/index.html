Nordic skiing, even without a signal

Deep in a Jura combe or a Queyras valley, the phone signal drops. The resort's trail map is a picture, the avalanche bulletin is on another website, and you are wearing gloves.

traceblanche puts the map of the area, its trails, its slopes, its rules and its huts on your phone before you leave. Everything stays readable offline. You plan the day in the morning; then a voice guides you while the phone stays in your pocket.

Where the project stands. The Rust core runs on two test sites, Cervières (Hautes-Alpes) and Chapelle-des-Bois (Doubs). The Android app is the next step. The images on this page come from the project's viewer, on real data.

What an offline pack holds

A pack covers one site: about 40 MB for Cervières, 35 MB for Chapelle-des-Bois. You download it before leaving. After that, the phone needs no network.

  • Two base maps as vector tiles: IGN Plan IGN v2 and OpenStreetMap.
  • Cross-country trails from OpenStreetMap: name, difficulty, classic or skating, direction.
  • Slopes steeper than 30°, computed from the IGN LiDAR HD terrain model at 5 m.
  • Avalanche exposure, modelled from terrain and forest with the AutoATES v3.0 and Flow-Py methods ported to Rust. The comparison with known avalanches is in progress.
  • Protected areas and their seasonal rules, huts from refuges.info, and areas with no mobile network at all, emergency calls included (ARCEP coverage maps).
  • Estimated snow from the Météo-France snowpack simulation, and the avalanche bulletin of the massif with its date, where Météo-France publishes one.
  • Voice clips recorded in advance: 884 clips for Cervières (4 MB), with the names of trails, huts and places.

traceblanche never says a slope is safe. It shows where the terrain gets steep, so that you decide before you get there. The avalanche bulletin and your own judgement on the spot remain the reference.

A route and its turnaround time

On 15 January in Cervières, the sun sets at 17:16. A loop that is too long ends in the dark.

The core computes a route on the trails, its length, its climb and its estimated time, classic or skating. It also gives the latest return time, 30 minutes before the end of civil twilight.

Where even emergency calls fail

An emergency call does not get through everywhere, and nothing on the ground tells you.

The pack marks the areas with no mobile network at all, 112 included, from the ARCEP coverage maps. You know before leaving where nobody can reach you, and you tell someone. In Cervières, these areas cover 51 km², nearly a quarter of the French part of the site.

The day, screen off

  • In the morning, one screen proposes the day, with the reason for each choice. You confirm it with one gesture. planned
  • During the outing, a voice gives directions, the time left and alerts: off route, slope over 30°, protected area, turnaround time, entering an area with no network. planned
  • With gloves on: touch targets of at least 72 dp, no fine gestures required, headset and volume buttons. planned
  • If you do not come back, an alert goes to the person you chose, even if your phone is dead. This service is optional. planned

Price

The intended price is a single payment, for example €4.99, with no subscription and no ads. The code is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later: you can also build the app yourself.

Contribute

Contributions are welcome: code, tests, data fixes, new sites. Contributions written with an AI tool are welcome too, when their author has reviewed them and brings the evidence: tests, command output, numbers. The project documents are in French.
